Interpretle modifications: export the text decoder through layer 21; retain
only the first 22 layer-specific embedding columns and projection rows; repack
the embedding columns into hashed static chunks; expose the gated MLP output;
quantize matrix weights to FP4 E2M1 with block size 32 and FP16 scales. These
approximate browser weights do not replace the original model's offline scores.
Normalization vectors and the selected SAE encoder retain their precision.
